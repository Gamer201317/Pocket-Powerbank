# Pocket Powerbank

Pocket is part of a modular power bank series.

## About Pocket

The Pocket Powerbank is a small power bank with great battery life. Since it is part of a modular power bank series, you can expand it with an expansion module, a wall plug module, and a hanger module with cables.

Its small form factor of **8.7 × 3 cm** allows you to carry it around easily. If the battery dies, you can simply buy a new 21700 battery, unscrew the cap, and replace the battery.

It also has a USB-C port with at least **18 W charging capability** and can fast-charge at up to **22.5 W**. If you're just carrying it around, press the on/off switch to save your battery.
